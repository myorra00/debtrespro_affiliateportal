```jsx
  We begin with a confidential discussion to understand your situation and MCA debt.
</ProcessStep>
<ProcessStep number="2" title="Comprehensive Analysis">
  Our experts analyze your MCA agreements and develop a tailored strategy.
</ProcessStep>
```

Lay out in a 4-column grid (stack on mobile).
