Section header — eyebrow overline + big display heading + optional intro. Opens every marketing section.

```jsx
<SectionHeading align="center"
  eyebrow="Our Services"
  title="Comprehensive Debt Resolution Services"
  intro="We leverage our expertise, negotiation skills, and commitment to integrity to deliver effective resolutions." />
```

`align="center"` for full-width sections; left for split layouts. The eyebrow gets the green→blue arc tick automatically.
