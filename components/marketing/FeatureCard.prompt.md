Service / value card — a filled icon tile, title, copy, and an optional "Benefits:" line. The building block of "Our Services" and "Why Choose DebtResolvePro" grids.

```jsx
<FeatureCard icon={<Scale/>} title="Debt Negotiation"
  benefits="Lower your outstanding balances and improve your cash flow.">
  Leverage our expert negotiation skills to communicate directly with your creditors and secure more favorable repayment terms.
</FeatureCard>
<FeatureCard tone="green" icon={<Handshake/>} title="Empathetic & Driven Partnership">
  We prioritize clear communication, transparency, and a supportive partnership throughout the process.
</FeatureCard>
```

Lay out in a 3-column grid. Alternate `tone` blue/green for rhythm.
